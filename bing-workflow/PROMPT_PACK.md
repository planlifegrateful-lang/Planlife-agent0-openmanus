# Bing Video Creator Prompt Pack — Plan Life Grateful

Use these as prompts for individual short clips. Generate each clip separately, then download the clips and number them in the intended sequence. Clip length and generation limits depend on Microsoft's current Bing experience.

## Universal visual style

Append this style to each scene prompt:

> Vertical 9:16 composition for a short-form video, cinematic but believable, clear central subject, strong visual contrast, smooth camera movement, realistic lighting, no visible text, no logos, no watermark, leave clean space near the top and bottom for captions. Keep important subjects centered so the video can be cropped to 9:16.

## Prompt A — Faceless motivation

1. **Hook:** A dramatic sunrise breaks through storm clouds over a long empty road, slow forward camera movement, cinematic realism, hopeful but intense atmosphere.
2. **The struggle:** Rain running down a window while a desk lamp illuminates an open notebook and a pair of worn shoes beside the door, quiet determination.
3. **Taking action:** Close-up of hands organizing a simple daily plan on blank paper, natural morning light, no readable writing.
4. **Turning point:** A dark cloud moves away as sunlight fills a quiet landscape, steady camera rise, symbolic change from uncertainty to hope.
5. **Payoff:** A winding road leading toward a bright horizon, wide cinematic shot, peaceful confidence.
6. **End frame:** Warm golden light on a calm landscape with generous negative space for a final caption.

Suggested overlay text to add later in an editor:
- “YOUR STORY ISN'T OVER.”
- “START WITH ONE SMALL STEP.”
- “KEEP YOUR FAITH. KEEP MOVING.”
- “PLAN LIFE GRATEFUL.”

## Prompt B — Product showcase (no unsupported claims)

Replace [PRODUCT] with the real product and use its actual packaging/reference image only if the tool supports it.

1. **Hook:** A premium macro shot of [PRODUCT] rotating slowly on a clean, neutral surface, crisp highlights and a fast cinematic push-in.
2. **Details:** Close-up of the actual packaging, label and texture, accurate proportions, studio lighting, no extra text or invented packaging.
3. **Context:** A tasteful, realistic tabletop scene showing [PRODUCT] beside relevant everyday items, no people or faces.
4. **Use case:** Show the product being used only in the manner specified by its official directions; avoid fabricated effects or before-and-after results.
5. **Payoff:** Clean hero shot of [PRODUCT], subtle camera orbit, high-quality commercial finish.
6. **CTA end frame:** Product centered with empty space for “Tap to learn more” to be added in editing.

Do not ask the video model to invent scientific results, customer reviews, badges, prices, discounts, or health benefits. Verify the real product label before writing the overlay.

## Prompt C — Islamic reminder

Create respectful, faceless visuals: dawn light outside a mosque, a peaceful prayer space with no identifiable people, rain easing over a quiet landscape, and sunlight spreading across a horizon. No invented Arabic calligraphy, Quran text, hadith, religious rulings, or sacred text in the generated image. Add any verified religious quotation manually only after checking its source.

Suggested overlay:
- “WHEN LIFE FEELS HEAVY…”
- “MAKE SPACE FOR PRAYER.”
- “KEEP DOING WHAT IS RIGHT.”
- “TAKE THE NEXT GOOD STEP.”

## 2-minute edit plan

Create and order enough clips to cover these beats:
- 0–5 seconds: hook / visual question
- 5–20 seconds: relatable situation
- 20–45 seconds: build curiosity
- 45–75 seconds: turning point or demonstration
- 75–100 seconds: useful lesson / verified product information
- 100–112 seconds: emotional or practical payoff
- 112–120 seconds: simple call to action

The local assembler gives all clips equal duration. For tighter story pacing, trim/reorder clips in an editor before or after assembly.
